MINA & MADONNA WEDDING INVITATION

Upload index.html to the root of your GitHub Pages repository.
For your photos to show, upload these image files to the same folder as index.html:
- childhood.jpg (childhood photo)
- couple.jpg (photo of the two of you)
- church.jpg (the exact church photo you want in the invitation)

The invitation works without these photos but shows a placeholder in their places.
Google Maps location is already linked. Dress code is shades from brown to yellow.
